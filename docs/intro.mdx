# Fitness Introduction

Let's discover **Proper fitness techniques in less than 5 minutes**.

## Getting Started

Get started by **warming up**.


### What fitness equipment will you need?

- mat
- weight training gloves
- fitness apparel
- foot pedals

## Why are warm-up exercises important?

Warming up before exercising helps your body get ready to be active by gradually and safely:

- increasing your body temperature
- increasing your heart rate
- increasing your blood pressure
- widening blood vessels

## What does this warm-up routine involve?

Round 1 exercises

For the first round, try these 4 lower body exercises. You can keep your hands by your side or on your hips as you perform each movement.

1. March on the spot

2. Foot pedals

3. Side taps

4. Knee raises 


## How do you know your ready to workout?

Repeat the exercises to make sure you’re warm after stretching.

Once you’ve completed these excercises, you’re ready to start your workout.
